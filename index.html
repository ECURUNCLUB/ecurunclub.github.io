Who we are

A run club that's really a friend group.

ECU Run Club started on a random Thursday in March 2026 when Louise had the bright idea of getting ECU students moving together. A few months later we're a proper crew, proudly funded by the ECU Guild, with new faces turning up every single week.

We loop the lake at a chatty pace, then head for free coffee. Along the way there've been themed runs, City to Surf, hikes, barbecues and a lot of group-chat memes.

How it works

Run. Walk. Coffee. Repeat.

  1. 01

    Rock up

    Meet us at Building 19, ECU Joondalup, just before 7am on Thursday. No sign-up fee, no experience needed.

  2. 02

    Run or walk

    About 5 km around the lake. Go fast, go slow, walk the whole thing. Nobody gets left behind.

  3. 03

    Free coffee

    Coffee after the run, thanks to our sponsors. This is the bit everyone really comes for.

  4. 04

    Repeat

    Same time next week, plus socials, themed runs, hikes and race days with the crew.
